Device **RTU-3 (10.0.0.5)** is unresponsive due to malicious traffic on port 2404. Flow data shows near-zero packet lengths (min=0), minimal inter-packet timing (IAT Min=239μs), and no forward PSH flags — consistent with a high-rate flood or SYN DoS starving the RTU.
