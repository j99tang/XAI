**Incident Briefing**

The RTU-1 device at Bus 2 in zone ZONE-N is under potential attack. The device controls breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, and BR-2-5. The observed network flow statistics indicate a possible denial-of-service or flooding attack, characterized by unusual packet timing and size patterns.

The immediate physical risk is the potential loss of supervisory control and visibility of the breakers, which could lead to uncontrolled grid operation at that substation. To mitigate this risk, I will immediately check the status of breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, and BR-2-5. If necessary, I will switch to manual control to ensure continuity of the physical process. I will also verify the communication status with RTU-1 and SCADA-1 to assess the extent of the potential disruption. Next steps will be taken to isolate the issue and prevent further disruption to the grid.
