**Incident Briefing**

Device affected: RTU-2 (10.0.0.3) at Bus 4, Zone ZONE-C, controlling breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9.

Physical risk: Potential loss of view and control of the breakers, or unintended switching, which could lead to grid instability or equipment damage.

Next actions:

1. Check the status of breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9 for any unusual activity.
2. Verify the integrity of RTU-2's connections and data exchange with SCADA-1.
3. Consider switching to manual control of the affected breakers to prevent potential damage or instability.
4. Investigate the network traffic pattern that triggered the alert, characterized by minimum backward inter-arrival time (Bwd IAT Min) and flow inter-arrival time (Flow IAT Min), and minimum packet lengths.

Prioritize safety and continuity of the physical process. Further analysis of the network traffic pattern will be conducted to determine the cause of the alert.
