**Incident Briefing**

Device affected: RTU-3 (10.0.0.5) at Bus 6, Zone ZONE-S, controlling breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13.

Physical risk: Potential loss of view and control of the breakers, or unexpected switching, which could lead to grid instability.

Analysis: The intrusion alert indicates a potential attack on RTU-3, with unusual network flow statistics, including low packet lengths and no forward PSH flags. This pattern suggests a possible flooding or denial-of-service attack.

Next actions:

1. Check the status of breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13 and verify their control functionality.
2. Switch to manual control of RTU-3 to prevent potential malicious actions.
3. Monitor the network flow statistics and RTU-3's performance closely for any further anomalies.
4. Prepare for possible grid instability and have contingency plans ready to ensure continuity of the physical process.
