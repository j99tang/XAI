**Incident Briefing**

Device affected: RTU-3 (10.0.0.5) at Bus 6, Zone ZONE-S, controlling breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13.

Observed pattern: Unusual network flow statistics, including low packet lengths and irregular inter-arrival times, indicating potential malicious activity.

Physical risk: Possible loss of view and control of breakers, or unintended switching, which could compromise grid stability and safety.

Next actions:

1. Check breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13 for any unusual status or activity.
2. Verify RTU-3's connection and response to SCADA-1.
3. Consider switching to manual control of affected breakers to ensure grid stability.
4. Monitor network traffic for further anomalies and investigate the cause of the unusual flow statistics.

Priority is to ensure continuity of the physical process and safety of the grid. Investigation into the cause of the incident will follow.
