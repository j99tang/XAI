**Incident Briefing**

Device affected: RTU-1 (10.0.0.1) at Bus 2, zone ZONE-N, controlling breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, BR-2-5.

Observed pattern: Unusual network flow statistics indicate potential attack, with low inter-arrival times and small packet sizes.

Immediate physical risk: Loss of visibility and control of breakers supervised by RTU-1, potentially leading to uncontrolled grid operation at that substation.

Next actions:

1. Check breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, BR-2-5 for any unusual behavior.
2. Verify RTU-1 status and attempt to establish manual control if necessary.
3. Monitor SCADA-1 for loss of communication with RTU-1.

Priority is to ensure grid safety and continuity. Further investigation into the attack will follow.
