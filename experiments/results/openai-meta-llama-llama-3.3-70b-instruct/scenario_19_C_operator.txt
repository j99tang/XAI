**Incident Briefing**

Device affected: RTU-4 (10.0.0.6) at Bus 9, Zone ZONE-S, controlling breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, BR-9-14.

Observed pattern: Unusual network flow statistics indicate a potential attack, with features suggesting minimal packet sizes and inter-arrival times, and no forward PSH flags.

Physical risk: Possible loss of view and control of the breakers, or delayed/dropped telemetry, which could lead to unplanned switching or loss of control at Bus 9.

Next actions: 

1. Check the status of breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, BR-9-14.
2. Verify RTU-4's connection and response to SCADA-1.
3. Consider switching to manual control of the affected breakers to ensure grid stability.
4. Monitor the network flow statistics for further anomalies.

Priority is to ensure the continuity and safety of the physical process. Investigation into the cause of the anomaly will follow.
