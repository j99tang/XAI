**Incident Briefing**

Device affected: RTU-4 (10.0.0.6) at Bus 9, controlling breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, BR-9-14.

Observed pattern: A single packet with a FIN flag has been sent to the RTU, with a relatively high flow byte rate (14733.6 bytes/s) and a long maximum inter-arrival time (17505).

Physical risk: Potential disruption to substation operations, potentially leading to loss of visibility and control of the breakers.

Next actions: 

1. Check the status of breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, BR-9-14.
2. Verify RTU-4's connection status and response to commands.
3. Prepare to switch to manual control if RTU-4 becomes unresponsive.
4. Monitor network traffic for further anomalies.

Priority is to ensure continuity of the physical process and safety of the substation. Further investigation into the cause of the alert will follow.
