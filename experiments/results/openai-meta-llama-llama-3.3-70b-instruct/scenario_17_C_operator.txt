**Incident Briefing**

Device affected: RTU-2 (10.0.0.3) at Bus 4, controlling breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9.

Physical risk: Potential loss of supervisory control and visibility of the breakers, but the grid will continue to operate.

Observed pattern: Unusual network flow statistics, including low Forward PSH Flags, high Packet Length Max, and low ACK Flag Count, indicating a possible flooding or denial-of-service attack.

Next actions: 

1. Check the status of breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9.
2. Verify RTU-2's communication with SCADA-1.
3. Consider switching to manual control of the affected breakers to ensure grid stability.
4. Monitor the network flow statistics for further anomalies.

Priority is to maintain grid stability and safety. Investigation into the cause of the anomaly will follow.
