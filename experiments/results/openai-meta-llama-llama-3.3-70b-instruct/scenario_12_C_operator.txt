**Incident Briefing**

Device affected: RTU-3 (10.0.0.5) at Bus 6, Zone ZONE-S, controlling breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13.

Physical risk: Potential loss of view and control of the breakers, leading to unobservable grid operation at that node.

Analysis: The intrusion alert indicates a possible attack on RTU-3, with unusual network flow statistics, including zero packet lengths and low inter-arrival times. This pattern suggests a flooding or denial-of-service attack.

Next actions:

1. Check the status of breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13.
2. Verify RTU-3's connectivity and response to commands.
3. Consider switching to manual control of the affected breakers to ensure grid stability.
4. Monitor the network traffic to RTU-3 for further anomalies.

Priority is to ensure the continuity of the physical process and safety of the grid. Investigation into the attack will follow.
