**Incident Briefing**

Device affected: RTU-4 (10.0.0.6) at Bus 9, controlling breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, and BR-9-14.

The network flow statistics indicate an unusual pattern, with a high Flow IAT Max and Flow Bytes/s, which may suggest a potential attack. The immediate physical risk is the potential loss of visibility and control of the breakers supervised by RTU-4.

Next actions:

1. Check the status of breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, and BR-9-14.
2. Verify the communication with RTU-4 and SCADA-1.
3. Consider switching to manual control of the affected breakers to ensure continuity of the physical process.
4. Monitor the network flow statistics for any further anomalies.

Priority is given to ensuring the safety and continuity of the physical process. Further investigation into the cause of the anomaly will be conducted after the immediate risks have been mitigated.
