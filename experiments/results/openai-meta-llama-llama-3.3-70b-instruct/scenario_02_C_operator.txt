**Incident Briefing**

Device affected: RTU-2 (10.0.0.3) at Bus 4, Zone ZONE-C, controlling breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9.

Physical risk: Potential loss of control and visibility of the substation, leading to unplanned switching or grid instability.

Analysis: The intrusion alert indicates a possible attack on RTU-2, with unusual network flow statistics, including low inter-arrival times and packet lengths. This pattern suggests a flooding or denial-of-service attack.

Next actions:

1. Check the status of breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9 for any unexpected changes.
2. Verify RTU-2's communication with SCADA-1 to ensure no loss of visibility or control.
3. Consider switching to manual control of the affected breakers to prevent potential damage or instability.
4. Monitor the network traffic for any further anomalies and prepare for potential grid instability.
