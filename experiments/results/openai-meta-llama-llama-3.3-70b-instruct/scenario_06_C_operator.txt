Device affected: RTU-2 (10.0.0.3) at Bus 4, Zone ZONE-C, controlling breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9.

Physical risk: Potential loss of view and control of the breakers, or unexpected switching, which could lead to grid instability.

Next actions: 

1. Check the status of breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9 for any unusual activity.
2. Verify the communication link between RTU-2 and SCADA-1.
3. Consider switching to manual control of the breakers to ensure grid stability.
4. Monitor the network traffic to RTU-2 for any further suspicious activity.

The observed network flow statistics indicate a potential attack, with features such as zero packet lengths and inter-arrival times, which may be indicative of a flooding or denial-of-service attack. Prioritize safety and continuity of the physical process.
