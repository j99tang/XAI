**Compliance Assessment**

This incident involves a potential cyber security incident affecting RTU-1, a BES Cyber Asset under NERC CIP. The intrusion alert indicates a high confidence level of an attack targeting the IEC-104 controlled station. Given the asset's critical role in controlling breakers, this incident plausibly meets the threshold for a Reportable Cyber Security Incident under NERC CIP-008-6.

The reporting timeline for this incident would be within one hour of determining a Reportable Cyber Security Incident, or by the end of the next calendar day if it is determined to be an attempted compromise. Notification is due to the E-ISAC and, under US jurisdiction, the NCCIC/CISA.

This response sits under the **Detect** and **Respond** functions of the NIST CSF 2.0 framework. Note that this assessment is illustrative, as our grid is a synthetic IEEE 14-bus overlay and not a jurisdictional determination under NERC CIP. Required attributes, including functional impact, attack vector, and level of intrusion, should be provided in the notification, even if some are unknown at the time of initial reporting.
