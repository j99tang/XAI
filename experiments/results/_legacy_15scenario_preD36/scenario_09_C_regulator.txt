**Regulatory Assessment**

Based on the provided context, I assess that this incident is reportable under NERC CIP-008-6.

The affected asset is RTU-4, an IEC-104 controlled station located at Bus 9, controlling breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, and BR-9-14. This device is a critical component of the grid's control system.

The incident involves small packets with headers dominating the traffic, which may indicate a flood/scan attack or a sustained bulk transfer. The presence of client packets carrying payload suggests a potential bulk command injection attack.

Given that RTU-4 is an IEC-104 endpoint and controls critical breakers, this incident meets the threshold for a reportable cyber security incident under NERC CIP-008-6.
