**Incident Briefing**

**Affected Device:** RTU-3, located at Bus 6, controls breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, and BR-6-13.

**Physical Risk:** The observed pattern of many small packets (headers dominate) indicates a potential flood/scan attack. This could lead to RTU connection table exhaustion, causing the system to become unresponsive or even crash.

**Immediate Action:**

1. Switch to manual control for RTU-3 to prevent further potential damage.
2. Check breaker BR-5-6 and its associated circuit breakers (BR-6-11, BR-6-12, and BR-6-13) for any signs of malfunction or unusual behavior.
3. Verify the status of all connected devices and circuits in Zone S.

**Next Steps:**

1. Investigate the source of the attack traffic to determine its origin and intent.
2. Review system logs and network traffic patterns to identify potential vulnerabilities or weaknesses that may have been exploited.
3. Collaborate with security teams to implement additional measures to prevent similar attacks in the future.

**Priority:** Safety and continuity of the physical process are paramount. Take immediate action to mitigate potential risks and ensure the stability of the grid.
