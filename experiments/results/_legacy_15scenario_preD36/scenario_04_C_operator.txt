**Incident Briefing**

Device Affected: RTU-1, Zone ZONE-N, Bus 2
Controlled Breakers: BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, BR-2-5

Observations:
* Sustained attack flow detected on RTU-1 (10.0.0.1) from IP range 121.142.26.0/24.
* Flow characteristics indicate a flood/scanning attack with many small packets and headers dominating the traffic.

Physical Risk: Potential disruption to grid operations if control system is compromised.

Immediate Actions:
1. Switch RTU-1 to manual control mode to prevent further potential disruptions.
2. Check breaker status for BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, and BR-2-5 to ensure they are in a safe state.
3. Verify that the grid is stable and no other devices are affected.

Next Steps:
* Investigate the source of the attack traffic and take necessary measures to prevent future incidents.
* Review and update security protocols for RTU-1 and other controlled devices.

**Switching Steps:**

1. Switch RTU-1 to manual control mode using the control panel or remote access.
2. Verify that the breakers are in a safe state by checking their status on the control panel or through remote access.
3. Monitor the grid for any further disruptions or anomalies.
