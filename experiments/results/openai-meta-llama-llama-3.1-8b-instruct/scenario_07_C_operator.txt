**Incident Briefing**

**Affected Device:** RTU-3 (10.0.0.5) at Bus 6, Zone ZONE-S

**Physical Risk:** The RTU is experiencing a high volume of small packets with headers dominating the traffic, indicating a potential flood or scanning attack. This could lead to RTU CPU/link exhaustion, delayed or dropped telemetry, and operator flying blind on that zone. Sustained flooding can force the RTU offline.

**Immediate Action:**

1. **Switch to manual control**: Immediately switch the affected breakers (BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13) to manual control to prevent any potential loss of control or unintended switching.
2. **Check RTU status**: Verify the RTU's status and performance to assess the impact of the attack.
3. **Monitor network traffic**: Continuously monitor the network traffic to identify any further anomalies or potential attacks.
4. **Notify relevant teams**: Inform the SCADA team and other relevant personnel about the incident and the actions taken.

**Next Steps:**

1. Investigate the source of the attack and take measures to prevent future incidents.
2. Review and update the incident response plan to ensure timely and effective response to similar incidents in the future.
