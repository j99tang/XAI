**Regulatory Assessment**

**Incident:** Predicted attack on RTU-3 (10.0.0.5) at Bus 6, controlling breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13, and BES Cyber Asset under NERC CIP.

**Reportability:** This incident plausibly meets the threshold for a reportable cyber security incident under NERC CIP-008-6. The RTU-3 is a BES Cyber Asset, and the predicted attack may compromise or disrupt its operation.

**Reporting Timeline:** The incident is an attempted compromise, so the next-day reporting timeline applies. The notification is due to the E-ISAC and, under US jurisdiction, the NCCIC/CISA, within one hour of determining the incident.

**Required Attributes:** The required attributes for the initial notification are functional impact, attack vector, and level of intrusion, which may be unknown at first notification. Updates will follow as more information becomes available.

**Framework Function:** This response sits under the **Detect** function of the NIST CSF 2.0 framework, as the incident was detected by the IDS system.
