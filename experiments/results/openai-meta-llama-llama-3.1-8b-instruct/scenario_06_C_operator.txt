**Incident Briefing**

**Device Affected:** RTU-2 (10.0.0.3) at Bus 4, Zone C, controls breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9, and BES Cyber Asset.

**Physical Risk:** The attack is causing a high volume of small packets to be sent to RTU-2, potentially overwhelming the device and disrupting control traffic. This could lead to a loss of view and control of the breakers, causing the grid to run but be unobservable at that node.

**Next Actions:**

1. Immediately switch to manual control of the breakers controlled by RTU-2 to prevent any potential disruptions.
2. Check the status of the breakers and the RTU-2 device to ensure they are functioning correctly.
3. Verify that the control traffic is being sent and received correctly.
4. Investigate the cause of the attack and take necessary measures to prevent similar incidents in the future.

**Priority:** Safety and continuity of the physical process take precedence over investigation. We must ensure the grid is running safely and reliably before we can investigate the cause of the attack.
