**Incident Briefing**

**Affected Device:** RTU-3 (10.0.0.5) at Bus 6, Zone ZONE-S

**Physical Risk:** The RTU is experiencing a potential denial of service (DoS) attack, which could lead to a loss of control over breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, and BR-6-13. This could result in a loss of power grid stability and potentially cause a blackout.

**Immediate Action:**

1. **Switch to manual control**: Immediately switch the affected breakers to manual control to prevent any potential damage or loss of control.
2. **Check breaker status**: Verify the status of breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, and BR-6-13 to ensure they are not malfunctioning or stuck in an open position.
3. **RTU-3 inspection**: Inspect RTU-3 for any signs of malfunction or unusual activity.
4. **Network traffic analysis**: Analyze network traffic to determine the source and nature of the attack.

**Next Steps:**

1. **Investigate the source**: Identify the source of the attack and take measures to prevent future incidents.
2. **Restore normal operation**: Once the attack is mitigated, restore normal operation of the RTU and breakers.
3. **Review and update procedures**: Review and update procedures to prevent similar incidents in the future.
