4. **Monitor System:** Continuously monitor the system for any changes or anomalies.

**Next Steps:**

1. **Investigate RTU-4:** Investigate the cause of the potential issue with RTU-4 and take corrective action.
2. **Review System Logs:** Review system logs to determine the source and nature of the potential attack.
3. **Notify Authorities:** Notify relevant authorities and stakeholders of the potential incident.

**Safety First:** Prioritize the safety of the grid and the public by taking immediate action to prevent any potential loss of control.
