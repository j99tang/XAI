**Incident Briefing**

**Device Affected:** RTU-1 (10.0.0.1) at Bus 2, Zone ZONE-N, controlling breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, BR-2-5.

**Physical Risk:** The attack may cause a loss of visibility and control of the breakers controlled by RTU-1, potentially leading to a grid instability or even a blackout. The immediate physical risk is the potential for a breaker to be opened or closed unexpectedly, causing a power outage.

**Next Actions:**

1. Switch to manual control for the breakers controlled by RTU-1 to prevent any potential unintended actions.
2. Check the breaker status and ensure they are in a safe state.
3. Verify the RTU-1's communication with the SCADA system and ensure it is functioning correctly.
4. Investigate the cause of the attack and take measures to prevent similar incidents in the future.

**Priority:** Safety and continuity of the physical process take precedence over investigation. We must ensure the grid remains stable and secure.
