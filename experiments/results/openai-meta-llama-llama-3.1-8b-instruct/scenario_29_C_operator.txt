**Incident Briefing**

**Affected Device:** RTU-4 (10.0.0.6) in Zone S, Bus 9, controlling breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, BR-9-14.

**Physical Risk:** The RTU is experiencing a sustained attack flow, which may lead to a denial of service (DoS) and loss of visibility and control of the substation's breakers. If the RTU is overwhelmed, it will stop responding, causing a full loss of control of the substation.

**Immediate Action:**

1. **Switch to manual control**: Immediately switch to manual control of the affected breakers (BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, BR-9-14) to prevent any potential loss of control.
2. **Check breaker status**: Verify the status of the breakers to ensure they are not malfunctioning or stuck in an open position.
3. **RTU status check**: Check the status of RTU-4 to determine if it is still operational and responding to commands.
4. **Investigate and isolate**: Investigate the source of the attack and isolate the affected RTU to prevent further damage.

**Next Steps:**

* Review the incident response plan and follow the procedures outlined in NIST SP 800-61r3.
* Notify the incident response team and other relevant personnel.
* Perform a thorough investigation to determine the root cause of the attack and implement measures to prevent similar incidents in the future.
