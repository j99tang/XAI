**Incident Briefing**

**Device Affected:** RTU-3 (10.0.0.5) at Bus 6, Zone ZONE-S, controls breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13.

**Physical Risk:** The attack traffic is directed at RTU-3, which controls critical breakers at Bus 6. If the RTU is compromised, it may lead to loss of control or unintended switching at the bus, potentially causing a power grid instability or even a blackout.

**Immediate Action:**

1. **Switch to manual control:** Immediately switch the control of the breakers to manual mode to prevent any potential unintended switching.
2. **Verify RTU status:** Check the status of RTU-3 to determine if it is still operational and communicating with the control center.
3. **Check breaker positions:** Verify the positions of the breakers controlled by RTU-3 to ensure they are not in an unexpected state.
4. **Isolate the RTU:** If the RTU is compromised, isolate it from the grid to prevent further potential issues.

**Next Steps:**

1. **Investigate the attack:** Once the immediate risk is mitigated, investigate the attack to determine the root cause and potential vulnerabilities exploited.
2. **Update the grid topology:** Update the grid topology to reflect any changes in the breaker positions or RTU status.
3. **Review and update security measures:** Review and update the security measures in place to prevent similar attacks in the future.

**Safety and Continuity:** The primary concern is the safety of the power grid and the continuity of the physical process. The immediate action is to switch to manual control and verify the RTU status to prevent any potential unintended switching or loss of control.
