**Incident Briefing**

**Device Affected:** RTU-2 (10.0.0.3) at Bus 4, Zone C

**Physical Risk:** The RTU is experiencing a high volume of small packets with no payload, indicating a potential flood or scanning attack. This could lead to RTU CPU/link exhaustion, delayed or dropped telemetry, and operator flying blind on that zone. Sustained flooding can force the RTU offline.

**Immediate Action:**

1. **Switch to Manual Control:** Immediately switch to manual control of the affected breakers (BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9) to prevent any potential loss of control or unintended switching.
2. **Check RTU Status:** Verify the status of RTU-2 and its communication with the SCADA system.
3. **Verify Breaker Status:** Confirm the status of the breakers under manual control to ensure they are in the correct position.
4. **Investigate Further:** Once the immediate risk is mitigated, investigate the source of the attack and take corrective action to prevent future incidents.

**Next Steps:**

* Check the RTU's communication with the SCADA system and verify that it is functioning correctly.
* Review the breaker status and ensure they are in the correct position.
* Investigate the source of the attack and take corrective action to prevent future incidents.

**Priority:** Safety and continuity of the physical process take precedence over investigation.
