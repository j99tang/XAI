**Regulatory Assessment**

**Incident Summary:** An intrusion alert was detected on RTU-3 (10.0.0.5), a BES Cyber Asset, with a prediction confidence of 1.00. The alert indicates a potential attack on the RTU, which controls breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13.

**Reportability:** The affected asset, RTU-3, is a BES Cyber Asset under NERC CIP. The incident plausibly meets the threshold for a Reportable Cyber Security Incident, as it involves a potential compromise of a BES Cyber System.

**Reporting Timeline:** The incident is reportable under NERC CIP-008-6, which requires notification within one hour of determining a Reportable Cyber Security Incident. Since the incident is still being analyzed, the one-hour timeline applies.

**Notification:** Notification is due to the E-ISAC and, under US jurisdiction, the NCCIC/CISA. The required attributes for the initial notification include functional impact, attack vector, and level of intrusion, which may be unknown at this time.

**Framework Function:** This response sits under the Detect function of the NIST CSF 2.0 framework, as the incident was detected by an IDS system.
