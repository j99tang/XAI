**Compliance Assessment**

The affected asset is RTU-2, a BES Cyber Asset under NERC CIP, which controls breakers at Bus 4. The intrusion alert indicates a prediction of an attack with a confidence level of 1.00, targeting the IEC-104 endpoint on port 2404.

This incident plausibly meets the threshold for a reportable cyber security incident under NERC CIP-008-6, as it involves a compromise or disruption of a BES Cyber System. The attack is directed at a critical control system, which could have significant consequences for the reliable operation of the Bulk Electric System.

**Reporting Timeline and Notification**

The reporting timeline applies to the initial notification, which must be made within one hour of determining a Reportable Cyber Security Incident. The notification is due to the E-ISAC and, under US jurisdiction, the NCCIC/CISA.

**Framework Function**

This response sits under the **Detect** capability, which is a function of the NIST CSF 2.0 (CSWP 29) framework. The incident response lifecycle, as outlined in NIST SP 800-61r3, will be followed to contain, eradicate, and recover from the attack.
