**Regulatory Assessment**

This incident is reportable under NERC CIP-008-6 as a potential compromise of a BES Cyber System. The affected asset, RTU-3 (10.0.0.5), is a critical component of the Bulk Electric System, controlling breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13, and BES Cyber Asset under NERC CIP. The intrusion alert indicates a prediction of an attack with high confidence, targeting the IEC-104 endpoint on port 2404.

**Reporting Timeline and Notification**

The incident occurred within the synthetic grid, which is illustrative of NERC CIP obligations. The reporting timeline applies as follows:

* Initial notification: within one hour of determining a Reportable Cyber Security Incident.
* For an attempted compromise: by the end of the next calendar day.

Notification is due to the E-ISAC and, under US jurisdiction, the NCCIC/CISA.

**Framework Function**

This response sits under the **Detect** capability of the NIST CSF 2.0 framework, which is responsible for identifying potential security incidents. The incident-response lifecycle follows the procedures outlined in NIST SP 800-61r3.
