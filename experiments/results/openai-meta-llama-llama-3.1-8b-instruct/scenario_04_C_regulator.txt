**Regulatory Assessment**

This incident is reportable under NERC CIP-008-6. The affected asset is RTU-4, a BES Cyber System, which is a critical component of the Bulk Electric System. The intrusion alert indicates a prediction of an attack with a confidence level of 1.00, targeting the RTU-4 at Bus 9. This plausibly meets the threshold for a Reportable Cyber Security Incident.

The reporting timeline applies as an initial notification is due within one hour of determining a Reportable Cyber Security Incident. The notification is due to the E-ISAC and, under US jurisdiction, the NCCIC/CISA.

This response sits under the **Detect** function of the NIST CSF 2.0 framework, which is responsible for identifying potential security threats. The incident handling lifecycle, as outlined in NIST SP 800-61r3, will be followed to contain, eradicate, and recover from the incident.
