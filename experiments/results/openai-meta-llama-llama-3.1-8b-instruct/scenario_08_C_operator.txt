**Incident Briefing**

**Device Affected:** RTU-4 (10.0.0.6) at Bus 9, Zone ZONE-S, controls breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, BR-9-14.

**Physical Risk:** The RTU is experiencing a high volume of small packets with irregular timing, indicating a potential flood or scanning attack. This could lead to RTU CPU/link exhaustion, delayed or dropped telemetry, and the operator flying blind on that zone.

**Immediate Action:**

1. Switch to manual control for RTU-4 to prevent potential RTU exhaustion or loss of view and control.
2. Check the breaker status and RTU-4's communication with the PLC to ensure no commands are being executed.
3. Verify the breaker positions and RTU-4's status to ensure the grid is safe and running.
4. Initiate a manual check of the RTU-4's communication with the PLC to confirm if the attack is still ongoing.

**Next Steps:**

1. Investigate the source of the attack and take necessary measures to prevent future incidents.
2. Review the RTU-4's communication logs to identify any potential security vulnerabilities.
3. Collaborate with the security team to implement additional measures to protect the grid from similar attacks.

**Safety First:** The immediate priority is to ensure the grid is safe and running, and the operator has a clear view of the situation.
