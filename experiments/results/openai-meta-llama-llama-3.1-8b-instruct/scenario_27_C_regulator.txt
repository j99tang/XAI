**Regulatory Compliance Briefing**

**Incident Analysis**

The affected asset is RTU-4, a BES Cyber System, which controls breakers at Bus 9. The intrusion alert indicates a potential attack on the RTU, which is an IEC-104 endpoint on port 2404.

**Reportability**

This incident plausibly meets the threshold for a reportable cyber security incident under NERC CIP-008-6. The attack on the RTU, a BES Cyber System, could compromise or disrupt the Bulk Electric System.

**Reporting Timeline**

The reporting timeline applies to the initial notification, which must be made within one hour of determining a Reportable Cyber Security Incident. Since the incident is still being analyzed, the one-hour timeline applies.

**Notification**

Notification is due to the E-ISAC and, under US jurisdiction, the NCCIC/CISA. The required attributes for the initial notification include functional impact, attack vector, and level of intrusion, even if some are unknown at first notification.

**Framework Function**

This response sits under the **Detect** capability, which is a part of the NIST CSF 2.0 framework. The incident analysis and reporting steps are guided by NERC CIP-008-6 and NIST SP 800-61r3.
