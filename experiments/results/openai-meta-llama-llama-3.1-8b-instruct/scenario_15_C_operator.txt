**Incident Briefing**

**Affected Device:** RTU-2 (10.0.0.3) in Zone C, Bus 4

**Physical Risk:** The RTU-2 is experiencing a potential attack, which may compromise its ability to control breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9. This could lead to a loss of control over these breakers, potentially causing a grid instability or even a blackout.

**Immediate Action:**

1. **Switch to Manual Control:** Immediately switch the affected breakers (BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9) to manual control to prevent any potential malicious actions.
2. **Verify RTU-2 Status:** Check the status of RTU-2 to determine if it is still operational and communicating with the control center.
3. **Check RTU-3 Status:** Verify the status of RTU-3 (10.0.0.5) in Zone S, Bus 6, as it is also connected to the same grid and may be affected by the attack.
4. **Monitor Grid Conditions:** Continuously monitor the grid conditions and breaker statuses to ensure the grid stability and prevent any potential cascading failures.

**Next Steps:**

* Investigate the cause of the attack and take corrective actions to prevent future incidents.
* Review the incident response plan and update it as necessary to ensure the grid's resilience against cyber threats.
