**Regulatory Assessment**

This incident triggers a reportable cyber security incident under NERC CIP-008-6. The affected asset, RTU-2, is a BES Cyber Asset under NERC CIP, controlling breakers at Bus 4. The intrusion alert indicates a prediction of an attack with high confidence, targeting RTU-2's IEC-104 endpoint on port 2404.

The attack plausibly meets the threshold for a reportable cyber security incident, as it compromises or disrupts a BES Cyber System. The required attributes for notification are functional impact, attack vector, and level of intrusion, which are unknown at this time.

The reporting timeline applies within one hour of determining the reportable cyber security incident. Notification is due to the E-ISAC and, under US jurisdiction, the NCCIC/CISA.

This response sits under the **Detect** capability of the NIST CSF 2.0 framework, which is mapped to the incident-response lifecycle in NIST SP 800-61r3. The incident is illustrative, as the grid is a synthetic IEEE 14-bus overlay and the traffic originates from a European testbed (SANDI-2024).
