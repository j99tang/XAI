3. **Verify breaker status**: Confirm the status of the breakers controlled by RTU-4 to ensure they are not malfunctioning or causing any safety issues.
4. **Monitor the situation**: Continuously monitor the RTU's performance and the breaker status to ensure the situation does not escalate.

**Next Steps:**

* Investigate the cause of the attack and take corrective action to prevent future incidents.
* Review the RTU's configuration and security settings to ensure they are up-to-date and secure.
* Consider implementing additional security measures to protect the RTU and the substation from similar attacks.
