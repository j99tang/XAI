ACTION BRIEFING — RTU-1 / Bus 2 / ZONE-N

Affected device: RTU-1, IP 10.0.0.1, GridCom GC-4000 IEC-104 controlled station at Bus 2. It supervises breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, BR-2-5.

This incident’s evidence: detector flagged attack traffic to 10.0.0.1:2404 with confidence 1.00. It saw only flow timing, sizes, and TCP flags: rapid small-packet pattern, packet length max 48, short inter-arrival times, no forward PSH flags. Protocol contents are not in context.

Immediate physical risk: loss of view/control for BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, BR-2-5. Breaker misoperation is not in context.

Next actions:

2. Check current status/indications for BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, BR-2-5.
3. Do not issue non-essential remote switching through RTU-1 until indications are confirmed stable.
