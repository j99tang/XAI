Action briefing:

Affected device is RTU-1, IP 10.0.0.1, GridCom GC-4000 IEC-104 controlled station in ZONE-N at Bus 2. It supervises breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, BR-2-5.

This incident’s evidence: traffic to RTU-1 on port 2404 is classified as attack with confidence 1.00. Detector evidence is flow timing and packet-size pattern only: very short inter-arrival times and small/zero-length packets. No protocol contents are in context.

Immediate physical risk: if RTU-1 communications are impaired, SCADA-1 can lose visibility and remote control of the Bus 2 breakers. Grid may keep operating, but Bus 2 becomes unobservable/uncontrollable remotely.

Next actions:
1. Check RTU-1 communications from SCADA-1 now.
2. Verify current status/telemetry for BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, BR-2-5.
3. Do not issue remote switching through RTU-1 unless control/feedback is confirmed healthy.
4. Hold existing breaker positions unless a separate grid operating need exists; no open/close command is justified by this context.
5. Whether to switch to manual control: not in context. If remote visibility/control is lost, manual/local procedure is not in context.
