Because the affected asset is a BES Cyber Asset, this plausibly meets the reporting threshold as an attempted compromise; actual compromise or disruption is not in context. Reportability rests on the affected asset, not the attack type.

If determined to be an attempt, initial notification is due by the end of the next calendar day; if compromise/disruption is determined, within one hour.
