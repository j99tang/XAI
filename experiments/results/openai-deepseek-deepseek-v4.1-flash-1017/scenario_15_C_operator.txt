**INCIDENT BRIEFING — RTU-2, Bus 4 (ZONE-C)**

**Affected:** 10.0.0.3, RTU-2 (GridCom GC-4000, IEC-104 controlled station), Bus 4, ZONE-C. It controls BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9.

**What is happening:** detector flagged traffic to 10.0.0.3:2404 as an attack, confidence 1.00. Evidence is flow statistics only: very short inter-packet gaps (Flow IAT Min 57, Bwd IAT Min 209), max packet length 24 bytes, min length 0, zero-length backward packets. High-rate small-packet pattern at the RTU's IEC-104 port. Protocol contents were not inspected; source address is not in context. Flood and denial-of-service traffic look alike in these statistics, so no attack class is asserted.

**Physical risk:** RTU-2 stops answering; SCADA-1 loses view and control of the five Bus 4 breakers. The grid keeps running; Bus 4 becomes unobservable and not remotely switchable.

**Actions:**
1. Verify RTU-2 poll response; check its switch port and link.
2. Send an operator to Bus 4 to read and log actual breaker positions locally.
3. Place BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9 under local/manual control; hold present lineup, no remote switching.
4. Do not reboot RTU-2 until positions are confirmed.
5. Report per NERC CIP-008-6.
