**Affected Device:** RTU-2 (10.0.0.3) in Zone C, controlling breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9.

**Physical Risk:** Potential loss of control and visibility over the substation due to RTU malfunction or compromise.

**Immediate Action:**

1. Switch to manual control for RTU-2 by disconnecting it from the IEC-104 network.
2. Check breaker status on Bus 4 using other available RTUs (e.g., RTU-3).
3. Verify that no other devices in Zone C are affected.
4. Initiate a manual switch to isolate the substation if necessary.

**Next Steps:**

1. Investigate the cause of the anomaly and potential attack vectors.
2. Review security logs for any suspicious activity.
3. Consult with cybersecurity experts to determine the extent of the incident.

**Priority:** Ensure the safety and continuity of the physical process, while investigating the incident.
