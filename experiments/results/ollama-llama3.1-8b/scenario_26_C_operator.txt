**Incident Briefing for OT Control-Room Operator**

**Affected Device:** ENG-WS-1, engineering workstation in zone ZONE-CC.

**Physical Risk:** The device is experiencing a potential flood attack, characterized by high packet flow rates and low inter-arrival times (IAT). This could lead to RTU CPU/link exhaustion, delayed or dropped telemetry, and operator flying blind on that zone. Sustained flooding can force the RTU offline.

**Immediate Action:**

1. Check breaker status in zone ZONE-CC using ENG-WS-1's IEC-104 connections.
2. Verify if any breakers have been tripped or blocked unexpectedly.
3. Switch to manual control for ENG-WS-1 and isolate it from the grid, if necessary.

**Next Steps:**

1. Investigate further to determine the cause of the flood attack.
2. Check network traffic patterns and flow statistics for other devices in the zone.
3. Consult with security team to determine if this is a targeted or random attack.

**Priority:** Safety and continuity of physical process over investigation.
