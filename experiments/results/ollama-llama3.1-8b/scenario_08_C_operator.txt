**Incident Briefing**

**Affected Device:** RTU-4, located at Bus 9, controls breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, BR-9-14.

**Physical Risk:** The observed pattern of small packets with zero bytes and high flow rates indicates a potential flooding attack. If this continues, it may cause RTU CPU/link exhaustion, leading to delayed or dropped telemetry, and potentially forcing the RTU offline.

**Immediate Action:**

1. Switch to manual control for RTU-4 to prevent further potential damage.
2. Check breaker BR-9-10 status; if it's not already tripped, trip it manually as a precautionary measure.
3. Verify the status of breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, and BR-9-14.

**Next Steps:**

1. Investigate the source of the attack traffic to prevent further incidents.
2. Review RTU-4's configuration and ensure it is up-to-date with the latest security patches.
3. Coordinate with the grid operator to assess the impact on the overall grid stability and take necessary measures to mitigate any potential consequences.

**Safety First:** Prioritize the safety of the grid and its operators; if necessary, consider temporarily isolating RTU-4 from the rest of the grid to prevent further damage.
