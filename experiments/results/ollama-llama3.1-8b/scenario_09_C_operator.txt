**Incident Briefing**

Device Affected: RTU-1, IP 10.0.0.1, Zone ZONE-N, Bus 2

Observations:

* High frequency of small packets with headers dominating traffic (Packet Length Max: 24, Packet Length Min: 0)
* Low inter-arrival time between packets in a flow (Flow IAT Min: 58, Bwd IAT Min: 243)
* No indication of large packets or sustained attack flows

Physical Risk:

* Potential loss of view and control of breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, BR-2-5
* Grid operation may be compromised if RTU-1 is overwhelmed or becomes unresponsive

Next Actions:

1. Immediately switch to manual control for RTU-1 to prevent further potential damage.
2. Check the status of breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, and BR-2-5 to ensure they are functioning correctly.
3. Verify that all other devices in Zone ZONE-N are operating normally.

**Switching Steps:**

* Switch RTU-1 to manual control using the ENG-WS-1 interface.
* Check breaker status using the RTU-1's IEC-104 protocol on port 2404.
* If necessary, manually switch breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, and BR-2-5 to a safe state.
