**Incident Briefing**

**Affected Device:** RTU-4, located at Bus 9, controls breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, BR-9-14.

**Physical Risk:** The observed pattern of small packets with headers dominating the traffic (Flow IAT Min: value=16) and high SYN Flag Count with low ACK Flag Count indicates a potential flood/scanning attack. This could lead to RTU CPU/link exhaustion, delayed or dropped telemetry, and operator flying blind on that zone.

**Immediate Action:** Switch to manual control for RTU-4 to prevent further damage. Check the breaker status at Bus 9 using BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, and BR-9-14 breakers' RTUs. Verify if any other devices in the zone are affected.
