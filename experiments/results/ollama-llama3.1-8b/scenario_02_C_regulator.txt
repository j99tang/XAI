**Regulatory Assessment**

This incident involves an intrusion alert on RTU-2, a BES Cyber Asset under NERC CIP, at Bus 4, controlling breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, and BR-4-9. The alert indicates a prediction of an attack with high confidence (1.00).

Based on the knowledge graph data, a Reportable Cyber Security Incident is triggered when there is a compromise or disruption of a BES Cyber System or its associated EACMS/PACS. Since RTU-2 is a BES Cyber Asset and the alert indicates a potential attack, it plausibly meets the threshold for a reportable incident.

The reporting timeline applies as follows: since this is an attempted compromise (not a successful one), notification must be made by the end of the next calendar day to NCCIC/CISA under US jurisdiction. The required attributes for initial notification include functional impact, attack vector, and level of intrusion, which may be unknown at first.

This incident response sits under the **Detect** capability of the CSF functions, as it involves detecting potential threats to the BES Cyber System.
