**Incident Briefing**

Device Affected: RTU-1, IP 10.0.0.1, Zone ZONE-N, Bus 2

Observations:

* High frequency of small packets with headers dominating traffic (Packet Length Min: 0, Packet Length Max: 24)
* Low inter-arrival time between packets in a flow (Flow IAT Min: 11)
* No indication of large packets or sustained attack flows
* Destination port is indicative of an IEC-104 endpoint

Physical Risk:

* Potential loss of view and control of breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, BR-2-5 due to RTU-1 malfunction or communication disruption

Next Actions:

1. Immediately switch to manual control for RTU-1 to prevent further potential disruptions.
2. Check breaker status and perform a manual check of the breakers controlled by RTU-1 (BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, BR-2-5).
3. Verify communication with RTU-1 and investigate the cause of the observed traffic pattern.

**Switch to Manual Control**
