**Incident Briefing**

Device Affected: RTU-4, located at Bus 9, controls breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, BR-9-14.

Physical Risk: The observed network flow statistics indicate a potential packet starvation attack on RTU-4. This could lead to the RTU's connection table being exhausted, causing it to stop answering SCADA commands and resulting in a full loss of visibility and control of that substation's breakers until the RTU is restarted.

Immediate Action: Switch to manual control for RTU-4 to prevent further potential damage. Check breaker BR-4-9, as it is directly controlled by RTU-4. Verify its status and ensure it is not affected by the potential attack.

Next Steps:

1. Check the status of breakers BR-7-9, BR-9-10, and BR-9-14 to confirm they are functioning correctly.
2. Switch to manual control for these breakers as well, if necessary.
3. Investigate further to determine the cause and scope of the potential attack.

**Safety First**: Prioritize maintaining grid stability and preventing any potential disruptions or losses of visibility and control.
