**Regulatory Assessment**

This incident involves an intrusion alert on RTU-2, a BES Cyber Asset under NERC CIP, at Bus 4, controlling breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, and BR-4-9. The alert indicates a prediction of an attack with high confidence (1.00).

Based on the knowledge graph data, this incident plausibly meets the threshold for a reportable cyber security incident under NERC CIP-008-6. A Reportable Cyber Security Incident involves a compromise or disruption of a BES Cyber System or its associated EACMS/PACS.

The reporting timeline applies as follows:

* Within one hour of determining a Reportable Cyber Security Incident.
* By the end of the next calendar day for a determination that an incident was an attempt to compromise an in-scope system.

Notification is due to both the E-ISAC and, under US jurisdiction, the NCCIC/CISA. The required attributes for notification include functional impact, attack vector, and level of intrusion.

This response sits under the **Detect** capability of the CSF functions, as outlined in NIST SP 800-61r3.
