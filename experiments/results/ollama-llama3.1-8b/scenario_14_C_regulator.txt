**Regulatory Assessment**

This incident involves an intrusion alert with a prediction of attack confidence of 1.00, targeting RTU-1 (10.0.0.1) at Bus 2, which controls breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, and BR-2-5. The affected asset is a BES Cyber Asset under NERC CIP.

Based on the knowledge graph data, a Reportable Cyber Security Incident is triggered when there is a compromise or disruption of a BES Cyber System or its associated EACMS/PACS. Since RTU-1 is a BES Cyber Asset and was targeted by an attack, this incident plausibly meets the threshold for reporting.

The reporting timeline applies as follows: since the incident involves an attempted compromise (not a successful one), the clock for initial notification is the next calendar day. The required attributes to be provided are functional impact, attack vector, and level of intrusion.

Notification is due to both the E-ISAC and NCCIC/CISA under US jurisdiction within the specified timeframe.

This response sits under the **Detect** capability of the CSF functions, as it involves identifying potential threats to the grid.
