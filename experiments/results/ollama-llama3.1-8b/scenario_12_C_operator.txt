**Incident Briefing**

**Affected Device:** RTU-3, 10.0.0.5, Zone-S, Bus 6
**Physical Risk:** Potential loss of control and view of breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13

The security alert indicates a potential attack on RTU-3. The observed pattern shows high `Bwd IAT Min` (0) and `Flow IAT Min` (258), indicating rapid packet exchange with short intervals. This is consistent with a flood or denial-of-service (DoS) attack, which could cause the RTU to become overwhelmed and lose control of its breakers.

**Immediate Action:**

1. Switch to manual control for Zone-S to prevent any potential loss of control.
2. Check breaker status for BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, and BR-6-13 to ensure they are not affected by the attack.
3. Verify that RTU-3 is functioning correctly and has not been compromised.

**Next Steps:**

1. Investigate the source of the attack traffic to determine its origin and intent.
2. Review the security logs for any suspicious activity or anomalies.
3. Consult with the security team to determine the best course of action to mitigate the threat and prevent future attacks.
