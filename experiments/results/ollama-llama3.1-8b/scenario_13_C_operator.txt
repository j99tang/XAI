**Incident Briefing**

Device Affected: RTU-4, located at Bus 9, controls breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-10, BR-9-14.

Physical Risk: The observed pattern of small packets with headers dominating the traffic indicates a potential flood/scanning attack. This could lead to delayed or dropped telemetry, forcing operators to fly blind on that zone and potentially causing unplanned switching or loss of control at its bus.

Immediate Action:

1. Switch RTU-4 to manual control mode.
2. Check breaker BR-9-10 for any unusual activity.
3. Verify the status of breakers BR-4-9, BR-7-9, BR-9-14.
4. Monitor network traffic for any signs of continued attack.

Next Steps:

1. Investigate and isolate RTU-4 to prevent further potential damage.
2. Review system logs for any suspicious activity leading up to the incident.
3. Consult with security team to determine the root cause and implement countermeasures.

**Safety First**: Ensure that all actions taken do not compromise the stability of the grid or put operators at risk.
