**Incident Briefing**

Device Affected: RTU-1, located at Bus 2 in Zone ZONE-N, controls breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, and BR-2-5.

Physical Risk: The observed pattern of small packets with headers dominating the traffic (small_packets) indicates a potential flood/scanning attack. If this continues, it may lead to packet starvation or loss of view and control of the breakers controlled by RTU-1.

Immediate Action:

1. Switch to manual control for RTU-1 to prevent any potential damage.
2. Check breaker status: BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, and BR-2-5 to ensure they are not affected by the potential attack.
3. Verify communication with RTU-1 to confirm it is still operational.

Next Steps:

* Investigate further to determine the source of the attack and its impact on the grid.
* Implement measures to prevent similar attacks in the future, such as network segmentation or intrusion detection systems.
* Document this incident for future reference and review.
