**Incident Briefing**

Device Affected: RTU-3, 10.0.0.5, Zone ZONE-S, Bus 6
Physical Risk: Potential loss of control and view of breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13

Observations:
* High frequency of small packets with headers dominating traffic (small_packets)
* Low packet length variance (repeated identical frames)
* Elevated flow packets per second (Flow Packets/s) and bytes per second (Flow Bytes/s)

Assessment: This pattern is indicative of a flood attack, potentially saturating the RTU. The high frequency of small packets suggests an attempt to overwhelm the device.

Next Steps:
1. Immediately switch to manual control for breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, and BR-6-13.
2. Check the status of RTU-3 and its communication with the PLC.
3. Verify that the grid is stable and no other devices are affected.

**Action Required:**

* Switch to manual control for breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, and BR-6-13
* Investigate RTU-3's status and communication with the PLC
