**Incident Briefing**

**Affected Device:** RTU-3, 10.0.0.5, Zone-S, Bus 6
**Physical Risk:** Potential loss of control and view of breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13

The security alert indicates a potential attack on RTU-3. The observed pattern shows high frequency of small packets with zero bytes, indicating a flood-like behavior. This is consistent with a Denial of Service (DoS) or flooding-type attack.

**Immediate Action:**

1. Switch to manual control for RTU-3 to prevent further potential damage.
2. Check the breaker status and ensure they are not being manipulated by the attacker.
3. Verify the communication link between RTU-3 and the SCADA system is secure.
4. Isolate RTU-3 from the network to prevent further attack.

**Next Steps:**

1. Investigate the cause of the attack and identify potential vulnerabilities in RTU-3's configuration or software.
2. Review the security measures in place for RTU-3 and ensure they are up-to-date and effective.
3. Coordinate with the SCADA team to verify the integrity of the system and prevent any further disruptions.

**Priority:** Safety and continuity of the physical process over investigation.
