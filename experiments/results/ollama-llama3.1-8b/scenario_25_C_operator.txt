**Incident Briefing**

**Affected Device:** RTU-2, 10.0.0.3, Zone C, Bus 4
**Physical Risk:** Potential loss of control and view of breakers BR-2-4, BR-3-4, BR-4-5, BR-4-7, BR-4-9

**Incident Analysis:**

The intrusion alert indicates a potential attack on RTU-2. The observed pattern shows high `Bwd IAT Min` and `Flow IAT Min`, indicating rapid packet exchange with short intervals between packets. This is consistent with a flood or denial-of-service (DoS) attack, where the attacker sends a large number of small packets to overwhelm the system.

**Immediate Action:**

1. Switch RTU-2 to manual control mode to prevent further potential damage.
2. Check breaker status and ensure they are not being manipulated by the attacker.
3. Verify that all other breakers in Zone C are functioning correctly.
4. Investigate and isolate the source of the attack.

**Next Steps:**

1. Check RTU-2's communication logs for any suspicious activity.
2. Review network traffic to identify potential entry points for the attack.
3. Consult with security team to determine the root cause and prevent future attacks.

**Priority:** Safety and continuity of physical process over investigation.
