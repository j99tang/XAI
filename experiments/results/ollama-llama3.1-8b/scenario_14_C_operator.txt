Device Affected: RTU-1, 10.0.0.1, Zone ZONE-N, Bus 2

Physical Risk: Potential loss of control and view of breakers BR-1-2, BR-2-3, BR-2-4, BR-2-5 due to possible attack on the RTU.

Next Actions:
