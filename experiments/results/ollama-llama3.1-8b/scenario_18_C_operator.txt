**Incident Briefing**

**Affected Device:** RTU-3, 10.0.0.5, Zone-S, Bus 6
**Physical Risk:** Potential loss of control and view of breakers BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, BR-6-13

**Incident Analysis:**

The intrusion alert indicates a potential attack on RTU-3. The observed pattern shows small packets with zero bytes, indicative of control traffic or a flood/scanning attack. The high frequency of small packets and low packet-length variance suggest a sustained attack flow.

**Immediate Actions:**

1. Switch to manual control for RTU-3 to prevent further potential damage.
2. Check the breaker status for BR-5-6, BR-6-11, BR-6-12, and BR-6-13 to ensure they are not affected by the potential attack.
3. Verify that the IEC-104 endpoint on port 2404 is functioning correctly.

**Next Steps:**

1. Investigate the source of the attack traffic and identify any potential vulnerabilities in RTU-3 or its communication protocols.
2. Review the security measures in place for RTU-3 and ensure they are up-to-date and effective.
3. Coordinate with the grid operators to assess the impact on the overall grid stability and take necessary precautions.

**Safety First:** Prioritize the safety of the grid and its operators, and take immediate action to prevent potential damage or loss of control.
