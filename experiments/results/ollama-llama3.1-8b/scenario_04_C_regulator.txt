**Regulatory Assessment**

This incident involves a potential cyber security threat to a BES Cyber Asset, specifically RTU-4 at Bus 9, which controls critical breakers in the grid. The detector has predicted an attack with high confidence (1.00) on destination IP address 10.0.0.6:2404.

Based on NERC CIP-008-6, a Reportable Cyber Security Incident is triggered when there is a compromise or disruption of a BES Cyber System or its associated EACMS/PACS. Since RTU-4 is a BES Cyber Asset and the incident involves potential unauthorized access to it, this plausibly meets the threshold for a reportable cyber security incident.

The reporting timeline applies as follows: since the incident is an attempted compromise (not a successful one), notification must be made by the end of the next calendar day. The required attributes for initial notification include functional impact, attack vector, and level of intrusion, which may not be fully known at this time.

Notification is due to both the E-ISAC and NCCIC/CISA under US jurisdiction. This assessment is illustrative, as our grid is a synthetic IEEE 14-bus overlay and CIP obligations are applied here for demonstration purposes only.
